V25 adds hero photo adjustment controls and a Preview button (owner only).
- Settings -> Titles & Text -> "Adjust the photo":
  * Move left/right and up/down (position)
  * Zoom (100-220%)
  * Brightness, Contrast, Color intensity (50-160% / 0-200%)
  * Reset adjustments
- "👁 Preview" hides the Settings window and jumps to the home page so you can
  see the banner full size; a floating "⚙ Back to Settings" button returns you
  with your unsaved changes intact.
- The photo is rendered on its own layer, so the adjustments and color filters
  never affect the hero title or text.
- Adjustments are saved to Firestore together with the photo (branding/hero
  {src, adj}) when you click Save Settings — readers see the exact same framing.
- Reopening Settings without saving discards unsaved photo changes (reloads the
  published version from Firestore).
- Cache/assets bumped to v25.
