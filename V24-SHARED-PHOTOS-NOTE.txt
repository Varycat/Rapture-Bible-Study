V24 moves the hero and study banner photos into Firestore so every reader sees them.
- New Firestore collection: branding
  * branding/hero            {src} — the hero background photo
  * branding/banner-<study>  {src} — one per study banner photo
- Only the owner can change them; every signed-in reader loads and sees them.
- The Settings photo controls (Hero background photo, Study Banner Photos) are
  now OWNER-ONLY and publish to Firestore when you click Save Settings.
- Readers no longer see those controls; their other appearance preferences
  (colors, fonts, layout) remain per-device as before.
- Old per-device photos from v22/v23 are ignored — set the photos once as the
  owner and Save Settings to publish for everyone.

of the /bibles and /notes rules from v20). Without it, Save Settings will show
