V29: study import & publishing, library filters & favorites, diagnostics.

1. IMPORT A BIBLE STUDY (Settings -> 📥, owner only)
   - Paste text, upload TXT / HTML / DOCX / PDF, or fetch a web page URL.
     (DOCX/PDF readers load on demand from cdnjs — mammoth and pdf.js.)
   - The app detects the title, splits sections into topics, finds Bible
     references (English and Spanish), and detects the language.
   - A preview shows everything found; "💾 Save as Draft" stores it in
     Firestore (studies/custom-... + a shared catalog in branding/catalog).
   - Drafts are visible ONLY to the owner (yellow DRAFT badge, Drafts filter).
   - Open a draft -> ✎ Edit Study now has Status (Draft/Published), Study
     language (EN/ES), and 🗑 Delete Study (custom studies only). Publishing
     makes it appear to readers you grant access to, like any other study.
   - Web-page import: many sites block direct download (CORS). The app says so
     honestly and suggests copy/paste instead.

2. LIBRARY FILTERS & FAVORITES
   - Filter chips above Featured Studies: All | English | Español | ★ Favorites
     (+ Drafts for the owner).
   - Every card has a ☆ star — favorites are saved to your account (Firestore
     progress doc) and follow you across devices.

3. PERFORMANCE & DIAGNOSTICS (Settings -> 🩺, owner only)
   - Real checks: network, service worker, app cache version, device storage
     used, Firestore offline cache, Bible cache, photo sizes (warns on large
     banners/hero), study counts, and JavaScript errors collected this session.
   - Safe one-click fixes: clear app cache & update; clear Bible device cache.
   - No fake diagnostics — every row is a real measurement.

4. STUDY BUILDER HELPERS
   - The topic editor's Additional Section now has a picker of ready-made
     bilingual section titles (Historical Context, Denominational Comparison,
     Seventh-day Adventist Interpretation, Objections, Study Questions,
     Sources, ...).

Cache/assets bumped to v29 (sw now precaches js/importer.js). No new Firestore rules needed.
