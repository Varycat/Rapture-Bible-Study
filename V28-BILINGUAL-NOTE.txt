V28: English/Español interface, floating-window theming, custom navigation links.

1. BILINGUAL INTERFACE (EN | ES)
   - Language selector in the top bar (EN/ES) and in Settings -> Layout & Display.
   - The choice is remembered on the device (survives refresh and reopening).
   - Translates: sign-in screen, top bar, sidebar, home page, study/topic
     buttons, the three interpretation cards, dialogs, Notes/Search/Bible
     floating windows, and the Settings window (sections, labels, buttons).
   - Study CONTENT (titles, teachings, verses) stays as authored by the owner —
     per-study translations are a future step.

2. FLOATING WINDOWS (Settings -> 🪟 Floating Windows)
   - Background, header, text, and border colors; border thickness; corner
     roundness; opacity (with blur); font size. Applies to Notes, Search, and
     Bible panels. Per-device like other appearance settings.

3. NAVIGATION LINKS (Settings -> 🧭 Navigation Links, owner only)
   - Add your own entries to the left menu: icon (emoji or image link), label,
     and what it opens — a study, a floating window, or any web link.
   - Duplicate, delete, reorder (up/down). Saved to Firestore with Save
     Settings, so every reader sees them.
