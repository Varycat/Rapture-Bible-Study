V37: translation is now AUTOMATIC for the owner.
- When the owner switches the language (EN <-> ES), the app automatically
  translates everything that is missing: study titles/subtitles/descriptions
  in the menu and library cards, and — when a study is open — all its topics.
- A small blue progress bubble at the bottom-left shows what is being
  translated; results save to Firestore as they finish, so readers see them
  too without any action.
- Opening a study while in the other language also auto-translates it.
- The manual "🌐 Translate this study" button remains (to force a re-run
  after editing the original text).
- Readers never trigger machine translation; they see the saved translations.
- Cache/assets bumped to v37.
