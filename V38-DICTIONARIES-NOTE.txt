V38: Bible dictionaries (English & Spanish).
- New floating 📕 Dictionary button: readers pick an installed dictionary and
  look up any word; accent-insensitive search; printable results. The panel
  defaults to the dictionary matching the current language.
- Settings -> 📕 Bible Dictionaries (owner only): upload dictionaries as
  JSON ({"term":"definition"} or a list of {term, definition}) or plain text
  ("TERM: definition" lines; multi-line definitions supported). Choose the
  name and language (English/Español) before uploading. Remove per dictionary.
- Stored in Firestore (dictionaries/{id}/chunks) so all readers get them;
  cached on-device after first load.
REQUIRED: publish the updated firestore.rules in the Firebase console
(adds the /dictionaries rules) — uploads are denied until you do.
